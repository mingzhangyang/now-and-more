时关于此时，还有

时间是确定的，下一块是什么，不确定。

「此时，还有」只有一页。上方是一座一秒不差的真时钟——时间在这里是确定的；下方是一张卡片，每分钟换一次，你永远不知道下一张会是什么。确定与偶然，就这样同处一页，各自安静。

拒绝信息茧房，拒绝信息焦虑。

这里没有信息流，没有推荐算法，没有红点和未读数；每分钟只递一张卡，抽到什么就是什么；世界很大，不必件件追赶。

你会在这里遇见

  • 与此同时——此时此刻，世界另一座城市的几点几分、日出日落，还有今晚的月亮；
  • 一首诗、历史上的今天、一个念头——旧文字在新时刻里重新出现；
  • 纯偶然——一个随机数、一把骰子、一枚硬币的正反，当场生成，只出现这一次；
  • 黄历与星象——按当天现场算出的老规矩与星位，仅供一乐；
  • 今日明信片——节气与节日那天，页面会送你一张画，配几行字；
  • 你自己的话——你可以随手写下一句心得或备忘，它会存进你的库，日后随机回来与你相遇；若选定了日子，它会在那天准时送达。

关于配比

每种内容出现的多少，是我们刻意的编辑选择：诗多一些，偶然少一些，热点只占一线。这个配比不随热度改变，也不为谁优化——它就是这件作品的口味。

关于你的东西

你写下的话、你添的日子、你的偏好，都只存在你自己的设备上，不上传、不跨设备、不属于任何人。细节写在隐私政策里。

愿你在这里，遇见一点意料之外。

时About Now, and More

The time is certain. What comes next is not.

Now, and More is a single page. At the top, a real clock, exact to the second — here, time is certain. Below it, one card that changes every minute, and you never know what the next one will be. Certainty and chance share the page, quietly.

No filter bubbles. No information anxiety.

There is no feed here, no recommendation algorithm, no badges or unread counts; one card a minute, whatever it happens to be. The world is wide, and you don't have to keep up with all of it.

What you may encounter

  • Meanwhile — the hour, sunrise and sunset of a city on the other side of the world, right now, and tonight's moon;
  • A poem, this day in history, a thought — old words arriving in a new moment;
  • Pure chance — a random number, a pair of dice, a coin's fall, generated on the spot and never repeated;
  • An almanac and the stars — the day's old rules and computed star positions, for delight only;
  • Today's postcard — on a solar term or a festival day, the page gifts you a painting with a few lines beneath it;
  • Your own words — you can leave a reflection or a memo; it joins your library and returns by chance on later days, and if you give it a date, it is delivered on that day, without fail.

About the mix

How often each kind of thing appears is a deliberate editorial choice: more poems, less chance, only a sliver of headlines. The mix does not follow trends and is not optimised for anyone — it is simply this work's taste.

About your things

The words you write, the days you add, and your preferences live only on your own device — never uploaded, never synced, never anyone else's. The details are in the privacy policy.

May you meet a little of the unexpected here.
