## House ruler weight audit

Every share is normalized by the candidate domain's total role weight and averaged over all sign/day/domain cases. `houseRulerParticipatingBodyShare` counts the full normalized weight of any body with positive house extras; it is not the increment. `houseRulerExtraShare` counts only explicit house-role bonuses, including those of newly admitted bodies. `houseRulerNewMembershipBaseShare` counts the base weight of house-only bodies, excluding their already-counted bonuses. `houseRulerIncrementalShare` is the sum of the latter two, without double counting. For D these are 33.212%, 3.483%, 4.003%, and 7.486%, respectively. A and C have zero house-related shares.

## Reproduction and validation

Run from a checkout containing the pinned commit: `mkdir -p backtest-output && TZ=UTC node scripts/horoscope-v3.2-backtest.cjs 2026-01-01 2031-01-01 > backtest-output/horoscope-v3.2-backtest.json`. The runner independently loads both revisions' solar helpers. `baselineSkyMs` measures baseline sampling; `baselineMs` includes both baseline scoring comparisons.

The tracked summary omits `signs`, `bodyContributions`, and `pairContributions` from each model; all other fields are retained unchanged. The full JSON report was regenerated by a full five-year Node 22 replay of the retrieved scoring/ephemeris source. All six overall star histograms and changed-star shares reproduce the original ablation. Runtime values are from the scoring-source replay, not a benchmark of the entire app. Direct checkout-based and Chromium validation were not available locally. The new `node --test tests/horoscope-v3.2-backtest.test.cjs` regression covers 23/25-hour civil days, lunar ingress, an omitted-ingress negative control, and the normalized role-weight decomposition; CI uses a checkout with Git history for the pinned baseline.
