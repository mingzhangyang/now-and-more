时隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 11 月 24 日

「此时，还有」是一个不需要你留下任何东西的网站。这一页把话说全：它存什么、不存什么、会向哪里发出请求。

没有账号

本站没有注册、没有登录、没有个人资料。你不需要提供姓名、邮箱或任何身份信息，就能使用全部功能。

没有分析、广告与追踪

本站自身不运行任何访问统计、广告或行为追踪：没有分析脚本，没有广告网络，没有第三方追踪器，也没有给任何人看的报表。

你的东西只存在你的设备上

为了让页面记得你的选择，少数数据保存在你自己的浏览器里，从不上传到任何服务器：

  • 偏好设置：语言、外观、时钟样式、你的星座、特效开关；
  • 「我的日子」里你添的纪念日；
  • 「写一句」的库——你写下的心得与备忘，存在浏览器的 IndexedDB 里；
  • 抽卡的去重进度与「第 N 次相遇」计数；
  • 经你允许取得的位置——只在本次浏览期间保存在本机（见下「位置」一节）。

这些数据只属于这台设备、这个浏览器。在浏览器的设置里清除本站的网站数据，它们会被一并删除，干干净净。

位置（可选，只为「星空」插画）

「星空」插画画的是某个地方今夜的天空。第一次画到这张卡时，页面会请浏览器允许使用你的位置——问与不问、许与不许，都由浏览器和你决定。若你允许，画的就是你所在之处的真实星空：位置只在你自己的设备上参与计算，绝不发送到任何服务器，也只在本次浏览期间记在这台设备上；纸面上只会写出离你最近的城市名（离哪座城市都不近时，就只写「你所在的地方」），从不写出坐标。若你拒绝，或浏览器无法提供位置，页面会改为随机选一座城市，画那里的星空，并在纸面上写明城市名——不会反复向你索取。

页面会发出的请求

本站发出的网络请求只有下面几类，都可以说明白：

  • 页面本身由 Cloudflare Pages 托管。托管平台在基础设施层面会有标准的技术日志（例如请求时间与地址），那是托管方的运维记录，不是本站在收集你的信息。
  • 字体与本站打包在一起，由提供页面的同一台服务器送达。本站不向任何第三方发出请求——没有例外。
  • 「此刻热点」的标题由本站自己的一个小服务（Worker）提供：它在服务器一侧从公开的新闻来源抓取最新标题，再交给你的页面。你的页面向它请求时，除任何网络请求都天然包含的信息（如 IP 地址）之外，不会发送任何关于你的数据——没有账号，没有标识符。分享图片与站点图标也由同一个服务提供。

联系

时Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Now, and More asks nothing of you. This page says the whole of it: what the site stores, what it does not, and where its requests go.

No account

There is no sign-up, no sign-in, and no profile. You never need to give a name, an email address, or any identifying information to use anything here.

No analytics, advertising, or tracking

The site itself runs no analytics, no advertising, and no behavioural tracking: no analytics scripts, no ad networks, no third-party trackers, and no reports for anyone.

Your things stay on your device

So the page can remember your choices, a few things are kept in your own browser and are never uploaded to any server:

  • Preferences: language, appearance, clock style, your zodiac sign, the effects switch;
  • The personal days you add under “My days”;
  • Your “Add a note” library — the reflections and memos you write, held in your browser's IndexedDB;
  • The no-repeat draw progress and the encounter counter;
  • A position you have allowed the page to use — kept on this device for this browsing session only (see “Location” below).

All of it belongs to this device and this browser only. Clear this site's data in your browser settings and everything goes with it, completely.

Location (optional, for the “Night Sky” illustration only)

The “Night Sky” illustration paints tonight's sky over a particular place. The first time such a card is drawn, the page asks your browser for permission to use your location — whether to ask, and whether to allow it, is for the browser and you to decide. If you allow it, the painting is the real sky above where you are: your position is used only in calculations on your own device, is never sent to any server, and is remembered only on this device for this browsing session. The paper names only the nearest city (or simply “Where you are”, when no city is near) — never coordinates. If you decline, or no position is available, the card instead picks a city at random, paints its sky, and names that city on the paper — and it will not keep asking.

The requests the page makes

The page makes only the requests below, and each can be accounted for:

  • The page itself is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like any host, Cloudflare keeps standard technical logs at the infrastructure level (such as request times and addresses); those are the host's operational records, not this site collecting information about you.
  • Fonts are bundled with the site itself and served by the same server that serves the pages. The site makes no third-party requests at all — no exceptions.
  • “Trending Now” headlines come from the site's own small service (a Worker): it fetches the latest headlines from public news sources on the server side and hands them to your page. When your page asks it for headlines, nothing about you is sent beyond what any web request inherently carries (such as an IP address) — no account, no identifier. The share image and site icons are served by the same service.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the site: contact@orangely.xyz.
